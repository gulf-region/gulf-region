Frequently Asked Questions

What is Monobenzone Cream 20% Iranian Brand?
Monobenzone Cream 20% Iranian Brand is Monozan®, manufactured by Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co. in Rasht, Iran. It contains monobenzone (monobenzyl ether of hydroquinone, or MBEH) at 20% concentration — the FDA-approved active ingredient for permanent depigmentation in extensive vitiligo, approved since 1952. It works by destroying melanocytes (pigment-producing cells) through free radical generation and tyrosinase inhibition, creating a uniform skin tone by removing remaining pigmented skin to match existing vitiligo patches. Monozan is the recognized pharmaceutical-grade equivalent of Benoquin, the original branded monobenzone cream that was discontinued for commercial reasons in the United States. Every batch ships with a Certificate of Analysis confirming concentration and quality, and GulfDermaCare delivers it to all GCC countries and worldwide with full customs documentation.

What is the difference between 20%, 40% and 60%?
Monobenzone 20%, 40%, and 60% all contain the identical active ingredient — the only difference is concentration, which affects speed rather than the final outcome. 20% is the standard starting strength, equivalent to the original discontinued Benoquin cream, and is recommended for all first-time users to assess skin tolerance. 40% contains double the concentration and typically produces visible results approximately 4-8 weeks faster, but carries a higher risk of skin irritation. 60% is the maximum available strength, reserved for advanced or treatment-resistant cases and requiring direct physician supervision. Nearly all dermatologists recommend beginning with 20% for at least 4-8 weeks before considering progression to a higher strength, since this allows proper assessment of individual skin sensitivity before committing to a more intense formulation that may be harder to discontinue comfortably if irritation develops.
