Monobenzone Availability in the USA

Monobenzone cream presents a significant availability challenge for US patients. Since Benoquin (the original branded monobenzone 20% cream) was discontinued by Valeant Pharmaceuticals, US patients have two main options:

Option 1: US Compounding Pharmacies

US compounding pharmacies can prepare monobenzone cream with a valid prescription from a licensed US physician. This is the traditional domestic route but comes with significant limitations:

  • Price: $150–$300+ per tube — significantly more expensive than international supply
  • Prescription required: A US-licensed physician must prescribe monobenzone cream
  • Limited availability: Not all compounding pharmacies offer monobenzone
  • Concentration limitation: Most US compounders only prepare 20% — 40% and 60% are rarely available

Is It Legal to Import Monobenzone into the USA?

The FDA's Personal Importation Policy (CPG 690.100) generally permits American individuals to import pharmaceutical products for personal use, subject to the following conditions:

  • The product is for personal use only (not for resale)
  • The quantity is reasonable for personal use (typically up to a 3-month supply)
  • The product does not represent an unreasonable risk
  • The product is not commercially promoted in the US
