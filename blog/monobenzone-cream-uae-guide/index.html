Monobenzone Cream Availability in UAE

The United Arab Emirates has a well-developed pharmaceutical sector, but monobenzone cream is not routinely available in UAE retail pharmacies due to its specialized prescription-only status and the discontinuation of the original Benoquin brand. Most UAE vitiligo patients seeking monobenzone cream source it through international supply.

UAE Customs Information

The UAE's Federal Authority for Nuclear Regulation and pharmaceutical import regulations require proper documentation for pharmaceutical imports. GulfDermaCare provides:

  • Certificate of Analysis from Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co.
  • Commercial invoice with pharmaceutical description and HS code
  • Packing list
  • Original manufacturer packaging

The vast majority of GulfDermaCare shipments to UAE clear customs without issues. In the rare event of a query, contact us via WhatsApp immediately and we will provide additional documentation.
