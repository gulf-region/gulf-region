What Is Iranian Brand Monobenzone?

When GCC dermatologists and vitiligo patients refer to "Iranian Brand Monobenzone Cream," they mean Monozan by Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co., manufactured in Rasht, Gilan Province, Iran. Behvazan produces Monozan to USP (United States Pharmacopeia) standards. The product has been distributed across GCC countries and recognized by regional dermatologists for its consistent quality. Monobenzone (monobenzyl ether of hydroquinone, MBEH) has held FDA approval for this specific indication since 1952, working through two combined mechanisms — generating free radicals that destroy melanocytes and simultaneously inhibiting tyrosinase, the enzyme responsible for melanin production. This dual action is why its effect is permanent, unlike hydroquinone-based treatments, which only suppress pigment temporarily and reverse once stopped. Available concentrations include 20% (the standard starting strength), 40% (accelerated results), and 60% (maximum strength, reserved for advanced cases under physician supervision).
