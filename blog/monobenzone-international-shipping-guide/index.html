Customs and Import Information

Monobenzone is a prescription pharmaceutical. Import regulations vary by country:

  • GCC countries: We provide full pharmaceutical documentation to support Gulf customs clearance. Most GCC shipments clear without issue.
  • USA: FDA personal importation policy generally permits importing a 3-month personal supply. Full documentation provided.
  • UK: MHRA personal importation policy allows personal-use import of prescription medicines with appropriate documentation.
  • Europe: Regulations vary by member state. Contact us for country-specific guidance.
📋 Customs assistance: If your shipment is held at customs, contact us immediately via WhatsApp. We will provide any additional documentation required to support clearance.
