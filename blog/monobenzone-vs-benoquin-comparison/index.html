Why Was Benoquin Discontinued?

Benoquin cream was manufactured by ICN Pharmaceuticals, acquired by Valeant Pharmaceuticals. The discontinuation was a commercial decision - not related to safety or efficacy. The FDA approval for monobenzone for vitiligo depigmentation remains active. The distinction matters because these agents are not interchangeable: monobenzone's permanent, irreversible effect makes it appropriate only for extensive vitiligo (50%+ body surface area) where a uniform depigmented result is the goal, while reversible options like hydroquinone or phototherapy remain first-line for limited vitiligo or non-vitiligo hyperpigmentation. A dermatologist can help determine which category fits a specific case before treatment begins. Both agents are manufactured to pharmaceutical standards when sourced correctly, with a Certificate of Analysis confirming active ingredient concentration for either product.
