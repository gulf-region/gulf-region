Understanding the Global Monobenzone Supply Chain

Since Benoquin (the original US-branded monobenzone cream) was discontinued, the global supply of pharmaceutical-grade monobenzone now comes from a small number of specialized manufacturers and compounding pharmacies worldwide. Iran-manufactured monobenzone, specifically Monozan® by Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co., has become one of the most widely distributed and trusted sources internationally. Monobenzone (monobenzyl ether of hydroquinone, MBEH) has held FDA approval for this specific indication since 1952, working through two combined mechanisms — generating free radicals that destroy melanocytes and simultaneously inhibiting tyrosinase, the enzyme responsible for melanin production. This dual action is why its effect is permanent, unlike hydroquinone-based treatments, which only suppress pigment temporarily and reverse once stopped. Available concentrations include 20% (the standard starting strength), 40% (accelerated results), and 60% (maximum strength, reserved for advanced cases under physician supervision).

Other Import Sources — What to Know

US Compounding Pharmacies

US compounded monobenzone requires a domestic prescription and typically costs $150-300+ per tube. Quality depends heavily on the individual compounding pharmacy's standards, since compounded medications are prepared to order rather than mass-manufactured under a single consistent GMP process.

Unbranded / Unknown-Origin Products

Some online marketplaces sell monobenzone-labeled creams without a clearly named manufacturer, GMP certification, or batch documentation. These carry significantly higher risk of incorrect concentration, contamination, or complete absence of the stated active ingredient.

How to Evaluate Any Monobenzone Source

Regardless of country of origin, look for these markers of a legitimate pharmaceutical source:

  • Named manufacturer: A specific pharmaceutical company you can verify, not just a generic "monobenzone cream" listing
  • GMP or equivalent manufacturing standard: Confirms consistent quality control
  • Batch-specific Certificate of Analysis: Available on request, confirming active ingredient concentration for your exact batch
  • Matching lot numbers: Box and tube should show the same batch/lot number
  • Valid expiry date: With reasonable shelf life remaining
  • Contactable supplier: Real customer support, not an anonymous storefront
