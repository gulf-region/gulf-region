Storage in Oman's Climate

Oman's summer temperatures can exceed 45°C in many regions. Store your monobenzone cream in an air-conditioned room below 25°C. Never leave in a hot vehicle or in areas without cooling. Do not refrigerate. The pharmaceutical shelf life is 2 years from manufacture when correctly stored.
