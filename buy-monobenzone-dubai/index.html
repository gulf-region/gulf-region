What Is Monobenzone Cream 20% Iranian Brand?

Monobenzone Cream 20% (Monozan®) is a pharmaceutical-grade topical cream manufactured by Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co. in Rasht, Iran. It contains monobenzone (monobenzyl ether of hydroquinone / MBEH) at 20% concentration — the FDA-approved active ingredient for permanent vitiligo depigmentation.

Often referred to as the "Iranian brand" monobenzone cream, Monozan® is the recognized pharmaceutical equivalent of the discontinued Benoquin cream. Dermatologists across UAE use monobenzone therapy for patients with extensive vitiligo covering 50% or more of body surface area.
