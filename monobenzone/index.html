Vitiligo Treatment & Monobenzone

Evidence-based information about vitiligo depigmentation therapy

What is Vitiligo?

Vitiligo (vit-ih-LIE-go) is a disease that causes loss of skin color in patches. The discolored areas usually get bigger with time. The condition can affect the skin on any part of the body, hair, and the inside of the mouth.

Two Vitiligo Treatment Approaches

1. De-pigmentation: Removing the remaining skin color of unaffected areas to match vitiligo patches. Recommended when vitiligo covers more than 50% of body surface.

2. Re-pigmentation: Recovering the skin color of affected areas using steroids, tacrolimus, NB-UVB, or PUVA therapy.

Monobenzone (MBEH) — The FDA-Approved Solution

Monobenzone (MBEH) is the only FDA-approved treatment for permanent depigmentation in vitiligo patients. It works by destroying melanocytes — the pigment-producing cells — through two mechanisms:

  • Free-radical formation: Permanently destroys melanocytes via oxidative stress
  • Tyrosinase inhibition: Blocks the enzyme responsible for melanin production
📚 Clinical evidence: A peer-reviewed study (PMC3533321) documented extremely satisfactory permanent depigmentation with monobenzone 20% cream within 3.5 months of twice-daily application, confirmed stable at 3-year follow-up.

Uses of Monobenzone Cream

  • Lightening normal skin to match vitiligo-affected areas
  • Permanent depigmentation when vitiligo covers 50%+ of body
  • Lightening surgical, acne, and injury scars
  • Treatment of melasma and hyperpigmentation
  • Reducing sun-induced dark spots and age spots
⚕️ Monobenzone causes permanent, irreversible depigmentation. Use strictly for vitiligo under dermatological supervision. Not for cosmetic skin lightening. Always use SPF 50+ sunscreen on treated areas.

🌍 Other Depigmentation Agents

Monobenzone is widely regarded as the most effective depigmenting agent. Other agents include:

  • Hydroquinone — temporary lightening, reversible
  • Mequinol — less potent, not FDA-approved for vitiligo
  • Tretinoin — used in combination for enhanced penetration
  • Azelaic acid — mild lightening for hyperpigmentation

Only monobenzone is FDA-approved for permanent vitiligo depigmentation.

Storage Instructions — Especially Important in GCC Climate

Proper storage is critical in the GCC region, where summer temperatures can exceed 45°C. Monobenzone cream stored incorrectly can degrade, reducing its therapeutic effectiveness.

🌡️
Temperature
Store below 25°C. Keep away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
💧
Humidity
Store in a cool, dry place. Do not store in bathrooms or humid areas.
❄️
Refrigerator
Do not refrigerate. Refrigeration can alter the cream base texture.

During summer months in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, we recommend storing your monobenzone cream in an air-conditioned room, away from windows. The product shelf life is typically 2 years from manufacture — we always ship products with a minimum of 12 months remaining.

Keep out of reach of children. Do not use after the expiry date printed on the packaging.
