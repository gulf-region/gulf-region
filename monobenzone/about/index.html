Who We Are

GulfDermaCare is the trading name of Skincare International, a licensed pharmaceutical exporter specialising in dermatological products for the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region.

We were established to address a significant gap in the market: the discontinuation of Benoquin cream (monobenzone 20%) left thousands of vitiligo patients in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain without access to their prescribed treatment. We source directly from Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co. in Rasht, Iran — the manufacturer of Monozan®, the pharmaceutical-grade equivalent of Benoquin.

Our Product

Our flagship product is Monobenzone Cream 20% Iranian Brand (Monozan® by Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co.). This is a pharmaceutical-grade topical cream containing monobenzone (MBEH) at 20% concentration — the FDA-approved active ingredient for permanent vitiligo depigmentation since 1952.

We also supply Monobenzone 40% and 60% for patients requiring higher concentrations, as well as Hydroquinone Cream USP and Tretinoin 0.1% cream.

Our Supplier — Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co.

Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co. is based in Rasht, Gilan Province, Iran. They manufacture Monozan® — the pharmaceutical-grade monobenzone cream that has been trusted by dermatologists across the Middle East and beyond for over a decade.

Behvazan operates under Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) standards with full batch documentation, independent quality testing, and a Certificate of Analysis issued for every production lot.

📄 A batch-specific Certificate of Analysis is included with every GulfDermaCare order — confirming the active ingredient concentration, manufacturing date, expiry date, and quality test results.

A Note on Medical Responsibility

Monobenzone cream is a prescription pharmaceutical indicated for permanent depigmentation in extensive vitiligo. GulfDermaCare operates as a pharmaceutical supplier, not a medical provider. We strongly recommend that all customers consult a qualified dermatologist before starting monobenzone therapy.
