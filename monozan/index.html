What is Monozan Cream?

Monozan is a brand name for monobenzone 20% cream manufactured by Behvazan Pharmaceutical Co., based in Rasht, Iran. Monozan cream contains monobenzone (monobenzyl ether of hydroquinone / MBEH) as the active pharmaceutical ingredient — the same active ingredient found in the well-known Benoquin cream.

Monozan is used exclusively for permanent depigmentation in vitiligo patients where vitiligo affects more than 50% of the body surface. It is FDA-approved active ingredient and has been used in clinical dermatology since 1952.

Monozan vs Benoquin vs Monobenzone

These are all the same treatment with different names:

  • Monobenzone — the active pharmaceutical ingredient (generic name)

Directions for Use

  1. Cleanse: Wash the treatment area with mild soap and lukewarm water; pat completely dry
  2. Apply thinly: Use a pea-sized amount per hand-sized area — a thin, even layer only
  3. Target correctly: Apply only to normally pigmented skin, not to existing vitiligo patches (which have no melanocytes to affect)
  4. Massage in: Gently rub until absorbed; do not apply a second layer
  5. Wash hands immediately: Within 2 minutes of application to avoid unintended depigmentation of palms
  6. Apply sunscreen: SPF 50+ broad-spectrum, 10-15 minutes after the cream is absorbed
  7. Repeat twice daily: Morning and evening, approximately 12 hours apart, without skipping applications
📋 Results typically become visible after 2-4 months, with complete depigmentation usually achieved within 9-12 months of consistent twice-daily use.

Storage Instructions

  • Store below 25°C in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Do not refrigerate
  • Keep the tube tightly closed when not in use
  • Keep out of reach of children
  • In hot climates (including GCC summer conditions), store in an air-conditioned room — never in a car or un-cooled space
  • Shelf life is 2 years from the manufacturing date shown on the packaging; use within 12 months of first opening

Warnings & Precautions

⚠️ Monozan causes permanent, irreversible depigmentation. This is not a cosmetic skin-lightening product and should only be used for its approved indication under dermatological supervision.
  • Not intended for cosmetic skin lightening, melasma, or age spots — consult a dermatologist about hydroquinone-based alternatives for these conditions
  • Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding without explicit physician guidance
  • Not routinely recommended for children under 12 without specialist supervision
  • Avoid contact with eyes, lips, and mucous membranes
  • Depigmented skin becomes permanently sun-sensitive — lifelong SPF 50+ sunscreen is mandatory on treated areas
  • Stop use and consult a dermatologist if you notice blue-black skin discoloration (a rare condition called exogenous ochronosis) or severe allergic reaction
  • Wash hands thoroughly immediately after every application to prevent unintended depigmentation

Who Should Consider Monozan

Monozan is specifically formulated for patients with extensive vitiligo — generally defined as vitiligo affecting 50% or more of total body surface area — who have either exhausted repigmentation options (topical steroids, NB-UVB phototherapy, calcineurin inhibitors) or made an informed decision that permanent depigmentation is the right path for their situation.

It is not intended for patients with limited, localized vitiligo where repigmentation therapies still offer a realistic chance of restoring natural pigment. A qualified dermatologist can help assess which category best describes your situation and whether Monozan is an appropriate next step.

Because the effect is permanent, most dermatologists recommend a thorough discussion of expectations, timeline, and lifestyle implications (particularly around lifelong sun protection) before beginning treatment — rather than starting based on cosmetic preference alone.
