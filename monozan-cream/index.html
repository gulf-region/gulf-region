What is Monozan Cream?

Monozan cream contains monobenzone (monobenzyl ether of hydroquinone / MBEH) as the active pharmaceutical ingredient — the same active ingredient found in the well-known Benoquin cream.

Monozan cream is used exclusively for permanent depigmentation in vitiligo patients. It is available in 20% concentration (standard strength) and 40% concentration (higher strength) — making monozan the most versatile monobenzone product available on the market today.

Monozan Skin Depigmentation Cream — How it Works

Monozan monobenzone cream achieves permanent skin depigmentation through a well-established mechanism:

  1. Free-radical cytotoxicity — destroys melanocytes (pigment cells) permanently via oxidative stress
  2. Tyrosinase inhibition — blocks the key enzyme in melanin production

The combined effect is gradual, permanent lightening of treated skin to match the depigmented vitiligo patches. Monozan/monobenzone is the most potent depigmenting agent available — the best depigmentation cream for full body vitiligo patients.

Directions for Use

  1. Cleanse: Wash the treatment area with mild soap and lukewarm water; pat completely dry
  2. Apply thinly: Use a pea-sized amount per hand-sized area — a thin, even layer only
  3. Target correctly: Apply only to normally pigmented skin, not to existing vitiligo patches (which have no melanocytes to affect)
  4. Massage in: Gently rub until absorbed; do not apply a second layer
  5. Wash hands immediately: Within 2 minutes of application to avoid unintended depigmentation of palms
  6. Apply sunscreen: SPF 50+ broad-spectrum, 10-15 minutes after the cream is absorbed
  7. Repeat twice daily: Morning and evening, approximately 12 hours apart, without skipping applications
📋 Results typically become visible after 2-4 months, with complete depigmentation usually achieved within 9-12 months of consistent twice-daily use.

Storage Instructions

  • Store below 25°C in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight
  • Do not refrigerate
  • Keep the tube tightly closed when not in use
  • Keep out of reach of children
  • In hot climates (including GCC summer conditions), store in an air-conditioned room — never in a car or un-cooled space
  • Shelf life is 2 years from the manufacturing date shown on the packaging; use within 12 months of first opening

Warnings & Precautions

⚠️ Monozan Cream causes permanent, irreversible depigmentation. This is not a cosmetic skin-lightening product and should only be used for its approved indication under dermatological supervision.
  • Not intended for cosmetic skin lightening, melasma, or age spots — consult a dermatologist about hydroquinone-based alternatives for these conditions
  • Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding without explicit physician guidance
  • Not routinely recommended for children under 12 without specialist supervision
  • Avoid contact with eyes, lips, and mucous membranes
  • Depigmented skin becomes permanently sun-sensitive — lifelong SPF 50+ sunscreen is mandatory on treated areas
  • Stop use and consult a dermatologist if you notice blue-black skin discoloration (a rare condition called exogenous ochronosis) or severe allergic reaction
  • Wash hands thoroughly immediately after every application to prevent unintended depigmentation
