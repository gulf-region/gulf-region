How to Verify Your Order Yourself

When your order arrives, you can independently confirm authenticity in under a minute:

  • Check that the lot/batch number on the box matches the number printed on the tube
  • Confirm the expiry date is clearly printed and has not passed
  • Message us your lot number via WhatsApp — we'll confirm it against Behvazan's production records
  • Request your Certificate of Analysis if it wasn't included, or if you'd like a copy for your own records

What Counterfeit or Unverified Products Look Like

Be cautious of any monobenzone product that:

  • Has no named manufacturer, or a manufacturer you cannot independently verify
  • Comes in generic, unbranded packaging with no batch number
  • Is priced suspiciously low (under $8 for a 30g 20% tube, including shipping)
  • Has no Certificate of Analysis available on request
  • Is sold by a supplier with no verifiable contact method or track record

Our Commitment

If you ever have doubts about the authenticity of a GulfDermaCare order, contact us immediately via WhatsApp with your batch number and order details. We will investigate and resolve any legitimate concern, including replacement where appropriate under our Return Policy.
