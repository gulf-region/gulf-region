What is MBEH Cream?

MBEH stands for Monobenzyl Ether of Hydroquinone — the chemical name for monobenzone. MBEH cream is pharmaceutical-grade monobenzone topical cream used for permanent depigmentation in vitiligo patients.

MBEH (monobenzone) is classified as the most potent depigmenting agent available, and has been FDA-approved since 1952 for permanent depigmentation of extensive vitiligo.

How MBEH Works

MBEH causes free-radical-mediated destruction of melanocytes and inhibits tyrosinase activity — permanently removing pigment from treated skin areas. The effect is irreversible as destroyed melanocytes cannot regenerate.

Directions for Use

  1. Cleanse: Wash the treatment area with mild soap and lukewarm water; pat completely dry
  2. Apply thinly: Use a pea-sized amount per hand-sized area — a thin, even layer only
  3. Target correctly: Apply only to normally pigmented skin, not to existing vitiligo patches (which have no melanocytes to affect)
  4. Massage in: Gently rub until absorbed; do not apply a second layer
  5. Wash hands immediately: Within 2 minutes of application to avoid unintended depigmentation of palms
  6. Apply sunscreen: SPF 50+ broad-spectrum, 10-15 minutes after the cream is absorbed
  7. Repeat twice daily: Morning and evening, approximately 12 hours apart, without skipping applications
📋 Results typically become visible after 2-4 months, with complete depigmentation usually achieved within 9-12 months of consistent twice-daily use.

Warnings & Precautions

⚠️ MBEH Cream causes permanent, irreversible depigmentation. This is not a cosmetic skin-lightening product and should only be used for its approved indication under dermatological supervision.
  • Not intended for cosmetic skin lightening, melasma, or age spots — consult a dermatologist about hydroquinone-based alternatives for these conditions
  • Not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding without explicit physician guidance
  • Not routinely recommended for children under 12 without specialist supervision
  • Avoid contact with eyes, lips, and mucous membranes
  • Depigmented skin becomes permanently sun-sensitive — lifelong SPF 50+ sunscreen is mandatory on treated areas
  • Stop use and consult a dermatologist if you notice blue-black skin discoloration (a rare condition called exogenous ochronosis) or severe allergic reaction
  • Wash hands thoroughly immediately after every application to prevent unintended depigmentation

Who Should Consider MBEH Cream

MBEH cream is formulated for patients with extensive vitiligo — generally 50% or more of body surface area — who have either exhausted repigmentation options or made an informed decision to pursue permanent depigmentation as their treatment path.

It is not a first-line treatment for limited or localized vitiligo, where repigmentation therapies (topical steroids, NB-UVB phototherapy, calcineurin inhibitors) still offer a realistic chance of restoring natural pigment in affected areas.

Given the permanent nature of MBEH's effect, a thorough conversation with a qualified dermatologist — covering realistic timelines, expected side effects, and the lifelong sun protection commitment — is strongly recommended before starting treatment.
